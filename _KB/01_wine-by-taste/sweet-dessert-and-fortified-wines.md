# Sweet, Dessert and Fortified Wines

## 1. What they are

Wines with **noticeable to high sweetness**, often concentrated and lush, plus **fortified** wines (grape spirit added), which range from bone-dry to very sweet. Customers say "dessert wine", "something sweet", "Port for after dinner", "sweet red", "wine for chocolate", "Moscato".

**Catalog:** `style_tag in (dessert, fortified, sweet-red)` · `sweetness in (medium_sweet, sweet)` (except dry Sherry/Madeira styles). Many come in **375 ml or 500 ml** bottles (count as 0.5 bottle toward the mixed case, A-50).

## 2. How sweetness is achieved

| Method | Examples | Taste |
|---|---|---|
| **Noble rot (botrytis)** | Sauternes, Barsac (Bordeaux); Tokaji Aszú (Hungary); Beerenauslese/Trockenbeerenauslese (Germany/Austria) | Honey, apricot, saffron, marmalade; high acidity keeps it fresh |
| **Late harvest** | Late Harvest Riesling/Gewürztraminer (US, Alsace "Vendanges Tardives") | Ripe peach, honey |
| **Frozen grapes (ice wine / Eiswein)** | Canada (Niagara), Germany, Finger Lakes | Intense, pure fruit, very sweet, high acid |
| **Dried grapes (passito)** | Vin Santo, Recioto, Passito di Pantelleria | Raisin, fig, nuts |
| **Fortification during fermentation** | Port, Vin Doux Naturel (Banyuls, Muscat de Beaumes-de-Venise) | Sweet, strong (15–20%) |
| **Fortification after fermentation / oxidative aging** | Sherry (Fino, Manzanilla, Amontillado, Oloroso dry; PX and Cream sweet), Madeira, Marsala | Nutty, savory to sweet |
| **Stopped fermentation (low alcohol)** | Moscato d'Asti, Brachetto d'Acqui, sweet Lambrusco | Light, fizzy, fruity, 5–8% |

## 3. Key wines

| Wine | Notes for the agent |
|---|---|
| **Ruby / Reserve Port** | Fruity, sweet red. Drink within ~4–6 weeks of opening. |
| **Tawny Port (10, 20, 30, 40 Year)** | Nutty, caramel. Age statement = average style, not minimum. Keeps 1–2 months open. |
| **Vintage Port / LBV** | Vintage Port is declared only in top years and needs decades; decant for sediment. LBV is ready to drink. |
| **Sauternes / Barsac** | Classic with foie gras, blue cheese, fruit tarts. |
| **Tokaji Aszú** | Sweetness historically shown in puttonyos; 5–6 puttonyos are very sweet. |
| **Ice wine** | Usually 375 ml; premium price due to tiny yields. |
| **Sherry** | Fino/Manzanilla: bone-dry, serve chilled, drink within days of opening. Oloroso: dry, nutty. Pedro Ximénez (PX): syrupy-sweet, pour over ice cream. |
| **Madeira** | Styles by grape: Sercial (driest), Verdelho, Bual, Malmsey (sweetest). Nearly indestructible once opened. |
| **Moscato d'Asti, Brachetto d'Acqui** | Light, low alcohol, sweet, fizzy. Brachetto is red, a great pairing with chocolate and berries. |
| **Sweet red blends (US)** | Popular, approachable; check `sweetness`. |

## 4. Indicative price bands

- **Everyday:** Ruby Port, Moscato, sweet red blends, Marsala, Cream Sherry.
- **Step-up:** 10-Year Tawny, LBV Port, Moscato d'Asti from top producers, Late Harvest Riesling (375 ml), Fino/Manzanilla.
- **Premium:** 20-Year Tawny, Sauternes, Tokaji Aszú, ice wine, vintage Madeira.
- **Luxury/Collectible:** Vintage Port from declared years, top Sauternes (e.g., first growth), 30/40-Year Tawny, very old Madeira.

## 5. How to recommend

- Ask: "With dessert, instead of dessert, or with cheese?" and "Light and fizzy, or rich and strong?"
- **Rule of thumb: the wine should be at least as sweet as the dessert.** Otherwise the wine tastes sour.
- **Chocolate**: Ruby/LBV Port, Banyuls, Brachetto d'Acqui.
- **Fruit desserts, cheesecake**: Moscato d'Asti, Late Harvest Riesling, Sauternes.
- **Blue cheese**: Port or Sauternes.
- **Caramel, pecan pie, nuts**: Tawny Port, Oloroso, Malmsey Madeira, PX.
- **Small households**: suggest 375 ml formats and long-lasting-after-opening styles (Tawny, Madeira).

## 6. Food pairing

See also [food-pairing-guide.md](food-pairing-guide.md). Serving temperature: sweet whites chilled 45–50°F; Ruby/Tawny Port slightly cool 55–60°F; Fino/Manzanilla well chilled; Vintage Port 60–65°F after decanting. Portions are small: 2–3 oz (60–90 ml).

## 7. Edge cases

- **"Fortified" = higher alcohol.** Mention ABV for customers watching alcohol (and see folder 05 for low-calorie questions; dessert wines are high in calories and sugar).
- **Vintage Port sediment** is normal; not a defect.
- **"Port" from outside Portugal**: EU rules protect "Port/Porto". US products may say "Port-style" or use a grandfathered term. Describe them as "port-style dessert wine".
- **Sweet wine and diabetes/health questions**: no health advice (G7). Point to product nutrition data if available and suggest asking a doctor.

## 8. Sample dialogue

> **Customer:** What should I serve with a chocolate lava cake?
> **Agent:** Chocolate needs a wine that's at least as sweet. A Ruby or LBV Port is the classic (rich berry and plum), or for something lighter and fizzy, try Brachetto d'Acqui, a sweet sparkling red that's lovely with chocolate. Here's what we have: … A 2–3 oz pour per person is plenty.
