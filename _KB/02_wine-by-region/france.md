# France: Regional Wine Guide

France labels by **place**. Knowing the region tells the customer the grapes and style. Champagne is covered in [champagne.md](../04_sparkling-champagne-prosecco/champagne.md).

## 1. Bordeaux (`France>Bordeaux`)

- **Grapes:** Red: Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Cabernet Franc (plus Petit Verdot, Malbec). White: Sauvignon Blanc, Sémillon.
- **Left Bank** (Médoc, Graves): Cabernet-led, structured, cassis, cedar, age-worthy. Key appellations: **Pauillac, Margaux, Saint-Julien, Saint-Estèphe, Pessac-Léognan**.
- **Right Bank**: Merlot-led, plusher, plum, truffle. **Saint-Émilion, Pomerol**.
- **Sweet:** **Sauternes, Barsac** (botrytized Sémillon).
- **Classifications:** 1855 Médoc (First to Fifth Growths) and Sauternes; Saint-Émilion (revised periodically); Graves. Many top estates are sold **en primeur (futures)** (see [pre-arrivals-futures-and-allocations.md](../09_stock-and-availability/pre-arrivals-futures-and-allocations.md)).
- **Price reality:** Huge range. Bordeaux Supérieur and Côtes de Bordeaux are Everyday/Step-up; classed growths are Luxury/Collectible.
- **Recommend for:** steak, lamb; Cab lovers wanting Old World; collectors.

## 2. Burgundy / Bourgogne (`France>Burgundy`)

- **Grapes:** Pinot Noir (red), Chardonnay (white). Gamay in Beaujolais (separate section).
- **Sub-regions:** **Chablis** (unoaked, crisp Chardonnay), **Côte de Nuits** (great reds: Gevrey-Chambertin, Vosne-Romanée, Chambolle-Musigny, Nuits-Saint-Georges), **Côte de Beaune** (great whites: Meursault, Puligny-Montrachet, Chassagne-Montrachet; reds: Pommard, Volnay), **Côte Chalonnaise** (value: Mercurey, Givry, Rully), **Mâconnais** (value whites: Mâcon-Villages, Pouilly-Fuissé, Saint-Véran).
- **Quality pyramid:** Bourgogne (regional) → Village → Premier Cru → Grand Cru (~1–2% of production).
- **Domaine vs négociant:** Both make excellent wine; producer matters more than tier.
- **Price reality:** Burgundy is scarce; top wines are allocated and expensive. Bourgogne Rouge/Blanc and Mâcon are the entry points.
- **Recommend for:** Pinot lovers, white Burgundy for lobster/roast chicken, special-occasion gifts.

## 3. Beaujolais (`France>Beaujolais`)

- **Grape:** Gamay. Light, juicy, low tannin.
- **Tiers:** Beaujolais Nouveau (released the 3rd Thursday of November), Beaujolais, Beaujolais-Villages, and **10 Crus**: Brouilly, Côte de Brouilly, Chénas, Chiroubles, Fleurie, Juliénas, Morgon, Moulin-à-Vent, Régnié, Saint-Amour.
- **Recommend for:** Thanksgiving, chillable reds, novices moving from white to red, value.

## 4. Rhône Valley (`France>Rhône`)

- **Northern Rhône:** Syrah reds (smoky, peppery, olive): **Côte-Rôtie, Hermitage, Crozes-Hermitage, Saint-Joseph, Cornas**. Whites: **Condrieu** (Viognier), Marsanne/Roussanne.
- **Southern Rhône:** Grenache-led blends (GSM): **Côtes du Rhône**, Côtes du Rhône Villages, **Gigondas, Vacqueyras, Rasteau, Châteauneuf-du-Pape**. Rosé: **Tavel**. Sweet: Muscat de Beaumes-de-Venise.
- **Price reality:** Côtes du Rhône is one of the best Everyday values; Châteauneuf and Hermitage are Premium–Luxury.
- **Recommend for:** smooth-but-flavorful reds, BBQ, stews, value seekers.

## 5. Loire Valley (`France>Loire`)

- **Sauvignon Blanc:** **Sancerre, Pouilly-Fumé, Touraine** (value).
- **Chenin Blanc:** **Vouvray** (sec to moelleux and sparkling), **Savennières** (dry, intense), Montlouis.
- **Melon de Bourgogne:** **Muscadet** (oyster wine).
- **Cabernet Franc reds:** **Chinon, Bourgueil, Saumur-Champigny**.
- **Sparkling:** **Crémant de Loire**, Vouvray Mousseux.
- **Recommend for:** crisp whites, seafood, goat cheese, lighter reds.

## 6. Alsace (`France>Alsace`)

- **Grapes:** Riesling, Gewürztraminer, Pinot Gris, Pinot Blanc, Muscat; labeled by grape (unusual for France). **Crémant d'Alsace** sparkling.
- **Style:** Dry to off-dry and sweet (Vendanges Tardives, Sélection de Grains Nobles). Sweetness can be hard to read; check product data or the sweetness scale some producers print.
- **Bottle:** Tall, slender "flûte".
- **Recommend for:** spicy food, aromatic-white lovers, pork, choucroute.

## 7. Provence (`France>Provence`)

- **Famous for pale, dry rosé** (Grenache, Cinsault, Syrah, Mourvèdre, Rolle/Vermentino): **Côtes de Provence**, Coteaux d'Aix-en-Provence, **Bandol** (powerful Mourvèdre reds and fuller rosé).
- **Recommend for:** summer, Mediterranean food, gifting (premium rosé brands).

## 8. Languedoc-Roussillon and the South-West

- **Languedoc-Roussillon:** Large, value-driven. **IGP Pays d'Oc** varietals, Corbières, Minervois, Saint-Chinian, Faugères, **Picpoul de Pinet**, Limoux (Crémant/Blanquette, possibly the oldest sparkling tradition), Banyuls (sweet fortified).
- **South-West:** **Cahors** (Malbec, "the black wine"), **Madiran** (Tannat), Jurançon (sweet and dry Petit/Gros Manseng), Gascogne whites (value).
- **Recommend for:** value bold reds, alternatives to Argentine Malbec.

## 9. Jura & Savoie (niche)

- **Jura:** Vin Jaune (oxidative, nutty), Savagnin, Poulsard, Trousseau; popular with natural-wine fans.
- **Savoie:** Alpine whites (Jacquère, Altesse), fondue wines.
