# Germany, Austria and Other Regions

## 1. Germany (`Germany`)

- **Regions:** **Mosel** (slate slopes; delicate, low-alcohol, often off-dry Riesling), **Rheingau** (fuller, often dry Riesling), **Pfalz** (warmer: dry Riesling, Pinot Noir = Spätburgunder), **Rheinhessen** (large, dynamic young growers), **Nahe**, **Baden** (Pinot family), **Franken** (Silvaner in the flask-shaped Bocksbeutel).
- **Grapes:** **Riesling** (signature), **Spätburgunder** (Pinot Noir), Silvaner, Weissburgunder (Pinot Blanc), Grauburgunder (Pinot Gris), Müller-Thurgau.
- **Reading sweetness** (most common customer confusion):
  - **Trocken** = dry. **Halbtrocken / Feinherb** = off-dry.
  - **Prädikat levels** (Kabinett, Spätlese, Auslese, Beerenauslese, Eiswein, Trockenbeerenauslese) describe **grape ripeness at harvest**. Kabinett and Spätlese without "trocken" are usually off-dry to sweet; BA, Eiswein and TBA are dessert wines.
  - **VDP Grosses Gewächs (GG)** = top dry wine from a top site.
- **Recommend for:** spicy food, sweet-wine lovers who want acidity, low-alcohol options (Mosel Kabinett is often 8–10% ABV).

## 2. Austria (`Austria`)

- **Grüner Veltliner:** green apple, white pepper, lentil; dry; versatile with vegetables. **Wachau** (Steinfeder → Federspiel → Smaragd, light → rich), **Kamptal**, **Kremstal**, **Weinviertel DAC**.
- **Riesling:** dry, intense (Wachau, Kamptal).
- **Reds:** **Zweigelt** (cherry, light, chillable), **Blaufränkisch** (dark, peppery; Burgenland), St. Laurent.
- **Sweet:** Burgenland (Neusiedlersee): botrytized wines, Ruster Ausbruch.
- **Recommend for:** "Sauvignon Blanc alternative", vegetarian meals, light reds.

## 3. Greece (`Greece`)

- **Assyrtiko (Santorini):** intensely mineral, high-acid white; premium seafood wine.
- **Xinomavro (Naoussa):** "Greek Nebbiolo": tannic, tomato, olive.
- **Agiorgitiko (Nemea):** soft, plummy red.
- **Moschofilero:** floral, light white. **Retsina:** pine-resin flavored, traditional.

## 4. Hungary (`Hungary`)

- **Tokaj:** **Tokaji Aszú** (botrytized, sweetness historically by puttonyos), **Tokaji Szamorodni**, and **dry Furmint** (steely, smoky).
- **Egri Bikavér** ("Bull's Blood"): red blend.

## 5. Eastern Mediterranean

- **Lebanon (Bekaa Valley):** Bordeaux/Rhône-style reds with spice; some long-established estates are well known.
- **Israel (Galilee, Golan Heights, Judean Hills):** a major source of quality **kosher** wine. Use `is_kosher=true`; "Kosher for Passover" and "Mevushal" (flash-pasteurized, relevant for catered events) are separate attributes.
- **Georgia:** ancient **qvevri** (clay vessel) tradition; amber/orange wines (Rkatsiteli), Saperavi reds.

## 6. England (`United Kingdom>England`)

- Fast-growing **traditional-method sparkling** from Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Pinot Meunier on chalk soils similar to Champagne's. Premium pricing. Good for "something different from Champagne".

## 7. Canada (`Canada`)

- **Ontario (Niagara Peninsula)** and **British Columbia (Okanagan Valley)**. Famous for **Icewine** (Vidal, Riesling, Cabernet Franc), usually in 375 ml. Also Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Riesling.

## 8. Other emerging regions

Uruguay (Tannat), Mexico (Valle de Guadalupe), Brazil (sparkling), Slovenia and Croatia (skin-contact whites, Plavac Mali), Switzerland (Chasselas; little exported), China (Ningxia), Japan (Koshu). Assortment is limited; check `search_products(country=…)`.
