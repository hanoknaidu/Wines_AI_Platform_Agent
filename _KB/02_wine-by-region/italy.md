# Italy: Regional Wine Guide

Italy grows hundreds of native grapes in 20 regions. Italian wine is food wine: high acidity, savory, built for the table. Prosecco is covered in [prosecco.md](../04_sparkling-champagne-prosecco/prosecco.md).

## 1. Piedmont (`Italy>Piedmont`)

- **Nebbiolo:** **Barolo** ("King of wines"; minimum 38 months aging, 18 in wood; Riserva 62 months) and **Barbaresco** (minimum 26 months; often a little more approachable). Pale color, high tannin and acidity; rose, tar, cherry. **Langhe Nebbiolo** is the value entry. Other Nebbiolo: Roero, Gattinara, Ghemme.
- **Barbera** (d'Asti, d'Alba): juicy, low tannin, high acid, an everyday classic.
- **Dolcetto**: soft, blackberry, early-drinking.
- **Whites:** **Gavi** (Cortese, crisp), **Arneis** (Roero), **Moscato d'Asti** (sweet, frizzante), **Asti Spumante** (sweet, fully sparkling).
- **Sweet red:** **Brachetto d'Acqui**.
- **Recommend for:** truffle, mushroom, braised meats (Barolo); pizza night (Barbera).

## 2. Tuscany (`Italy>Tuscany`)

- **Sangiovese family:** **Chianti** and **Chianti Classico** (black rooster seal; Annata → Riserva → Gran Selezione), **Brunello di Montalcino** (100% Sangiovese, long aging), **Rosso di Montalcino** (value "baby Brunello"), **Vino Nobile di Montepulciano** (Sangiovese; not the Montepulciano grape), **Morellino di Scansano**.
- **Super Tuscans / Bolgheri:** Cabernet, Merlot and blends, often IGT Toscana or Bolgheri DOC. Polished, international style; some are icon wines.
- **Whites:** **Vernaccia di San Gimignano**, Vermentino (coast).
- **Sweet:** **Vin Santo**.
- **Recommend for:** red-sauce pasta, steak Florentine, gifts (Brunello, Super Tuscans).

## 3. Veneto (`Italy>Veneto`)

- **Valpolicella family** (Corvina blends): Valpolicella (light), **Valpolicella Ripasso** (richer), **Amarone della Valpolicella** (dried grapes, 15%+, rich, raisin/chocolate), **Recioto** (sweet).
- **Whites:** **Soave** (Garganega), **Pinot Grigio** (delle Venezie DOC), **Lugana** (Turbiana).
- **Sparkling:** **Prosecco** DOC/DOCG.
- **Recommend for:** Amarone as a luxury gift and with aged cheese; Pinot Grigio as a crowd-pleaser.

## 4. Northeast: Alto Adige, Trentino, Friuli

- **Alto Adige / Südtirol:** Alpine, crisp: Pinot Grigio, Pinot Bianco, Gewürztraminer, Sauvignon, Lagrein and Schiava reds. Cooperative wineries offer great quality and value.
- **Trentino:** **Trento DOC** traditional-method sparkling.
- **Friuli:** Friulano, Ribolla Gialla, orange wines (skin-contact), Pinot Grigio with more texture.

## 5. Lombardy

- **Franciacorta:** Italy's top traditional-method sparkling (see [sparkling-styles-and-production-methods.md](../04_sparkling-champagne-prosecco/sparkling-styles-and-production-methods.md)).
- **Lugana** (shared with Veneto), Valtellina (Nebbiolo/Chiavennasca).

## 6. Central Italy

- **Abruzzo:** **Montepulciano d'Abruzzo** (soft, plummy, outstanding value), Trebbiano d'Abruzzo, Cerasuolo (rosé).
- **Marche:** **Verdicchio** (crisp, almond).
- **Umbria:** Sagrantino (very tannic), Orvieto (white).
- **Emilia-Romagna:** **Lambrusco** (sparkling red; secco to dolce).

## 7. Southern Italy and the islands

- **Campania:** **Aglianico** (Taurasi), whites Fiano di Avellino, Greco di Tufo, Falanghina.
- **Puglia:** **Primitivo** (Zinfandel's twin), Negroamaro; ripe, value-driven.
- **Basilicata:** Aglianico del Vulture.
- **Sicily:** **Nero d'Avola** (plush), **Frappato** (light), **Etna Rosso** (Nerello Mascalese, elegant, volcanic), **Etna Bianco** (Carricante), Grillo, Marsala (fortified).
- **Sardinia:** **Cannonau** (Grenache), **Vermentino di Gallura**.

## 8. Label tips

- Many labels are **grape + place** (Barbera d'Asti = Barbera grape from Asti) or **place only** (Barolo = Nebbiolo).
- **DOCG** neck strip = top category (government seal), but DOC/IGT wines can be just as good.
- **Montepulciano** confusion: grape (Abruzzo) vs town (Vino Nobile, Tuscany).

## 9. Indicative price bands

- **Everyday:** Montepulciano d'Abruzzo, Pinot Grigio, Primitivo, Nero d'Avola, Lambrusco, basic Chianti.
- **Step-up:** Chianti Classico, Barbera d'Alba, Langhe Nebbiolo, Rosso di Montalcino, Ripasso, Etna Rosso, Gavi.
- **Premium:** Barolo/Barbaresco entry, Brunello, Amarone, Chianti Classico Gran Selezione, Franciacorta.
- **Luxury/Collectible:** Top Barolo crus, Brunello Riserva, icon Super Tuscans.
