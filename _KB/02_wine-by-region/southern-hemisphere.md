# Southern Hemisphere Regional Guide

**Vintage note:** Southern Hemisphere harvests happen around **February–April**, so a "2025" Southern wine was picked about six months before a "2025" Northern wine. New vintages of crisp whites (e.g., NZ Sauvignon Blanc) often arrive in US stores mid-year. Adjacent-vintage substitution rules still apply (A-22).

## 1. Argentina (`Argentina`)

- **Mendoza** (sub-regions **Luján de Cuyo**, **Uco Valley**: Tupungato, Tunuyán, San Carlos): high-altitude vineyards. Intense sun, cool nights, ripe fruit with freshness.
- **Malbec:** plum, blackberry, violet, cocoa; plush, smooth-ish tannins. Argentina's signature and a top value bold red. Uco Valley versions are fresher and more floral.
- **Also:** Cabernet Sauvignon, Bonarda (soft, value), Cabernet Franc (rising), **Torrontés** (Salta/Cafayate: floral, dry white), Chardonnay.
- **Patagonia** (Neuquén, Río Negro): cooler; Pinot Noir, Malbec.
- **Recommend for:** steak, burgers, BBQ, empanadas; "smooth bold red under $20".

## 2. Chile (`Chile`)

- **Valleys run east–west** from the Andes to the Pacific; Chile labels coastal (Costa), middle (Entre Cordilleras) and Andes zones.
- **Maipo** (Cabernet, near Santiago), **Colchagua** and **Cachapoal** (Rapel Valley: Cabernet, Carménère, Syrah), **Casablanca** and **San Antonio/Leyda** (cool coastal: Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay, Pinot Noir), **Maule** and **Itata** (old-vine País, Carignan, Cinsault), **Aconcagua**, **Limarí** (Chardonnay).
- **Carménère:** Chile's signature, rediscovered in the 1990s after being mistaken for Merlot. Plum, red pepper, spice.
- **Recommend for:** value Cabernet, Sauvignon Blanc, smooth reds; Carménère for adventurous drinkers.

## 3. Australia (`Australia`)

- **South Australia:** **Barossa Valley** (rich Shiraz, old vines, Grenache), **McLaren Vale** (Shiraz, Grenache, Mediterranean grapes), **Clare Valley** and **Eden Valley** (dry Riesling: lime, steely), **Coonawarra** (Cabernet; terra rossa soil), **Adelaide Hills** (cool: Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay, Pinot).
- **Victoria:** **Yarra Valley** and **Mornington Peninsula** (Pinot Noir, Chardonnay), Rutherglen (fortified Muscat, "stickies").
- **Western Australia:** **Margaret River** (Cabernet, Chardonnay, Sémillon-Sauvignon blends).
- **New South Wales:** **Hunter Valley Sémillon** (lean young; toasty with age).
- **Tasmania:** cool-climate sparkling, Pinot Noir.
- **"South Eastern Australia"** is a huge multi-state GI used by large value brands.
- **Recommend for:** bold Shiraz, value whites, crowd-pleasing brands, dry Riesling.

## 4. New Zealand (`New Zealand`)

- **Marlborough:** **Sauvignon Blanc**: intense grapefruit, passion fruit, fresh herbs, crisp. The benchmark for that style and one of the most-requested whites in US stores. Also Pinot Noir and Pinot Gris.
- **Central Otago:** world's southernmost commercial wine region; vibrant **Pinot Noir**.
- **Hawke's Bay:** Bordeaux blends, Syrah, Chardonnay.
- **Martinborough / Wairarapa:** Pinot Noir.
- **Screwcaps:** the large majority of NZ wine uses screwcaps, a quality choice, not a cheap one.
- **Recommend for:** zesty whites, seafood, Pinot Noir lovers seeking fruit-forward elegance.

## 5. South Africa (`South Africa`)

- **Wine of Origin (WO)** regions: **Stellenbosch** (Cabernet, Bordeaux blends, Pinotage), **Swartland** (old-vine Chenin Blanc, Rhône blends; dynamic producers), **Constantia** (Sauvignon Blanc; historic sweet Vin de Constance), **Walker Bay / Hemel-en-Aarde** (Pinot Noir, Chardonnay), **Franschhoek**, **Paarl**, **Elgin**.
- **Chenin Blanc ("Steen"):** South Africa has more Chenin than any other country; dry, fresh to rich and oaked.
- **Pinotage:** a South African cross (Pinot Noir × Cinsault); smoky, dark fruit, sometimes "coffee" style.
- **Méthode Cap Classique (MCC):** traditional-method sparkling.
- **Recommend for:** value Chenin, adventurous reds, Bordeaux-style reds at lower prices.

## 6. Indicative price bands

- **Everyday:** Malbec, Chilean Cabernet/Carménère/Sauvignon Blanc, Australian brands, Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc (many), South African Chenin.
- **Step-up:** Uco Valley Malbec, top Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc, Barossa Shiraz, Margaret River Chardonnay/Cabernet, Central Otago Pinot entry, Swartland blends.
- **Premium:** Single-vineyard Malbec, top Barossa/McLaren Vale, top Chilean icons, Central Otago reserve Pinot.
- **Luxury/Collectible:** Australian icon Shiraz, Chilean and Argentine icon blends.
