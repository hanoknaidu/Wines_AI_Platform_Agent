# Spain and Portugal: Regional Wine Guide

The Iberian Peninsula is one of the best sources of **value** in wine, plus world-class fortified wines (Sherry, Port, Madeira).

## SPAIN

### 1. Rioja (`Spain>Rioja`, DOCa)

- **Grapes:** Tempranillo-led, with Garnacha, Graciano, Mazuelo (Carignan). White Rioja: Viura.
- **Aging tiers** (red; legal minimums): **Joven/Genérico** (little or no oak), **Crianza** (2 years, ≥ 1 in oak), **Reserva** (3 years, ≥ 1 in oak), **Gran Reserva** (5 years, ≥ 2 in oak). Newer labels also highlight zones (Rioja Alta, Rioja Alavesa, Rioja Oriental) and single vineyards (Viñedo Singular).
- **Style:** Traditional producers use **American oak**: vanilla, dill, coconut, leather. Modern producers use French oak and riper fruit.
- **Recommend for:** lamb, roast meats, tapas; Gran Reserva for aged-wine lovers at a fair price.

### 2. Ribera del Duero, Toro, Priorat

- **Ribera del Duero:** Tempranillo (Tinto Fino), bolder and darker than Rioja. Same aging terms.
- **Toro:** Very powerful Tempranillo (Tinta de Toro).
- **Priorat (DOQ):** Garnacha and Cariñena on slate (llicorella) soils: concentrated, mineral, premium.
- **Recommend for:** Napa Cab drinkers wanting Spain; steak.

### 3. Other Spanish reds

- **Garnacha** regions (Calatayud, Campo de Borja, Navarra): ripe, value.
- **Monastrell** (Jumilla, Yecla): dark, warm, value.
- **Mencía** (Bierzo, Ribeira Sacra): fresh, floral, medium-bodied, a "Pinot-lover's Spanish red".

### 4. Spanish whites

- **Albariño (Rías Baixas):** peach, saline, crisp. Seafood.
- **Verdejo (Rueda):** citrus, herbal, value.
- **Godello (Valdeorras):** textured, between Chardonnay and Albariño.
- **Txakoli (Basque):** very light, slightly spritzy, low alcohol.

### 5. Cava (sparkling)

- **Traditional method**, mostly Macabeo, Xarel·lo, Parellada (plus Chardonnay, Pinot Noir). Mostly Penedès, Catalonia.
- Minimum aging 9 months on lees; **Cava de Guarda Superior** (Reserva 18+ months, Gran Reserva 30+ months, Paraje Calificado 36+ months).
- **Recommend for:** best-value traditional-method bubbles; mimosas; parties. See folder 04.

### 6. Sherry (Jerez) (`Spain>Andalucía>Jerez`)

- **Dry:** **Fino** and **Manzanilla** (aged under flor yeast; pale, saline, almond; serve chilled; drink within days of opening), **Amontillado** (flor then oxidative; nutty), **Oloroso** (oxidative; walnut, rich but dry), **Palo Cortado** (rare, between Amontillado and Oloroso).
- **Sweet:** **Pedro Ximénez (PX)**, **Moscatel**, **Cream** (blended sweet).
- Solera aging system. See [sweet-dessert-and-fortified-wines.md](../01_wine-by-taste/sweet-dessert-and-fortified-wines.md).

## PORTUGAL

### 7. Douro and Port (`Portugal>Douro`)

- **Douro DOC reds:** Touriga Nacional, Touriga Franca, Tinta Roriz (Tempranillo): dark, structured, floral; great value premium.
- **Port** (fortified, sweet): Ruby, Reserve, **LBV** (Late Bottled Vintage), **Vintage** (declared years; long aging; decant), **Tawny** (10/20/30/40 Year; nutty, caramel), Colheita (single-year tawny), White and Rosé Port (cocktails, Port & tonic).

### 8. Vinho Verde (`Portugal>Minho`)

Light, crisp, often slightly spritzy, low alcohol whites (Loureiro, Alvarinho, Arinto). Alvarinho from Monção e Melgaço is the premium tier. Also rosé and red versions.

### 9. Alentejo, Dão, Bairrada, Lisboa

- **Alentejo:** warm, ripe, plush reds; value.
- **Dão:** elegant Touriga Nacional, Encruzado whites.
- **Bairrada:** Baga reds (tannic, age-worthy) and sparkling.

### 10. Madeira

Fortified and deliberately heated (estufagem or canteiro) during aging, so it is extraordinarily long-lived once opened. Styles: Sercial (dry) → Verdelho → Bual → Malmsey (sweet). Also Rainwater (lighter, medium-dry).

## Indicative price bands

- **Everyday:** Garnacha, Monastrell, Rioja Crianza (many), Verdejo, Vinho Verde, Cava, Alentejo reds, Ruby Port.
- **Step-up:** Rioja Reserva, Albariño, Mencía, Ribera Crianza, Douro reds, LBV, 10-Year Tawny, Fino/Manzanilla.
- **Premium:** Rioja Gran Reserva, Priorat, Ribera Reserva, Cava Gran Reserva, 20-Year Tawny.
- **Luxury/Collectible:** Icon Ribera and Priorat, Vintage Port from top years, old Madeira.
