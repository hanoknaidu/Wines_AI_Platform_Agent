# USA, California: Regional Wine Guide

California produces the large majority of US wine and is the most-requested region in US stores. Labels are mostly by **grape**; the **AVA** says where it was grown (≥ 85% from that AVA).

## 1. Napa Valley (`USA>California>Napa Valley`)

- **Signature:** **Cabernet Sauvignon**: rich cassis, black cherry, mocha, polished tannins, often new French oak. Also Merlot, Cabernet Franc, Bordeaux blends, Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc.
- **Nested AVAs (16+):** Oakville, Rutherford ("Rutherford dust"), Stags Leap District, St. Helena, Calistoga, Howell Mountain, Mount Veeder, Spring Mountain District, Diamond Mountain District, Atlas Peak, Coombsville, Yountville, Oak Knoll, Chiles Valley, Wild Horse Valley, and Los Carneros (shared with Sonoma).
- **Mountain vs valley floor:** Mountain fruit is more structured and tannic; valley floor is plusher.
- **Price reality:** Napa is expensive. Under $30 Napa Cab is rare; many famous wines are Premium–Collectible and some are allocated/mailing-list only.
- **Recommend for:** gifts, steak dinners, celebrations, "big red" lovers.

## 2. Sonoma County (`USA>California>Sonoma County`)

- **Russian River Valley:** Pinot Noir and Chardonnay (cooler, foggy).
- **Sonoma Coast / West Sonoma Coast:** very cool, high-acid Pinot and Chardonnay.
- **Alexander Valley:** Cabernet Sauvignon (softer than Napa, good value).
- **Dry Creek Valley:** **Zinfandel**, Sauvignon Blanc.
- **Carneros:** Pinot, Chardonnay, sparkling.
- **Recommend for:** Pinot Noir and Chardonnay lovers; Napa-style Cab at a lower price (Alexander Valley).

## 3. Central Coast (`USA>California>Central Coast`)

- **Paso Robles:** Cabernet, Rhône varieties (Syrah, Grenache, Mourvèdre), Zinfandel; warm, ripe, excellent value. Has 11 sub-AVAs.
- **Santa Barbara County:** **Sta. Rita Hills** (Pinot Noir, Chardonnay; cool), **Santa Maria Valley**, **Santa Ynez Valley / Ballard Canyon** (Syrah).
- **Monterey / Santa Lucia Highlands / Arroyo Seco:** Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Riesling.
- **Edna Valley, Arroyo Grande:** cool Pinot and Chardonnay.
- **Recommend for:** value Cab and Rhône blends (Paso), elegant Pinot (Sta. Rita Hills).

## 4. Other California regions

- **Lodi:** Old-vine **Zinfandel**; many value brands. "LODI RULES" sustainability certification (see folder 06).
- **Mendocino / Anderson Valley:** Pinot Noir, Gewürztraminer, sparkling; organic pioneers.
- **Sierra Foothills (Amador, El Dorado):** Zinfandel, Barbera.
- **Livermore Valley, Santa Cruz Mountains:** historic Cabernet and Chardonnay; Santa Cruz Mountains is a high-quality niche.
- **"California" appellation:** grapes from anywhere in the state. Typical of large value brands.

## 5. California styles at a glance

| Grape | California style | Best AVAs |
|---|---|---|
| Cabernet Sauvignon | Ripe, full, oaky, polished | Napa (Oakville, Rutherford), Alexander Valley, Paso Robles |
| Chardonnay | From buttery/oaky to lean/cool | Russian River, Sonoma Coast, Carneros, Sta. Rita Hills, Napa |
| Pinot Noir | Riper, cola, cherry; cooler sites more elegant | Russian River, Sonoma Coast, Sta. Rita Hills, Santa Lucia Highlands, Anderson Valley |
| Zinfandel | Jammy, peppery, high alcohol | Dry Creek, Lodi, Paso Robles, Amador |
| Sauvignon Blanc | Riper (melon, citrus); "Fumé Blanc" = oaked | Napa, Sonoma, Lake County |
| Red blends | Smooth, fruit-forward, sometimes off-dry | Statewide |
| Sparkling | Traditional method, Champagne-house-owned estates | Carneros, Anderson Valley, Sonoma |

## 6. Indicative price bands

- **Everyday:** "California" appellation brands, Lodi Zinfandel, Central Coast Chardonnay and red blends.
- **Step-up:** Paso Robles Cab, Sonoma Chardonnay, Russian River Pinot entry, Alexander Valley Cab, California traditional-method sparkling.
- **Premium:** Napa Valley Cab, top Sonoma Pinot, Sta. Rita Hills.
- **Luxury/Collectible:** Napa sub-AVA and cult Cabernets, single-vineyard Pinot.

## 7. How to recommend

- **Napa Cab under budget**: try Sonoma (Alexander Valley), Paso Robles or Washington (see [usa-pacific-northwest-new-york-and-new-jersey.md](usa-pacific-northwest-new-york-and-new-jersey.md)), or a Napa producer's second label.
- **"Like Rombauer (buttery Chard)"**: `get_similar_products`; Carneros/Napa/Russian River oaked styles.
- **"Like Meiomi (Pinot)"**: fruit-forward, smooth, slightly sweet-fruited Coastal California Pinot. See [us-brands.md](../03_wine-by-brand/us-brands.md).
- **Collectors**: confirm vintage (vintage-specific, A-23), storage history and allocation limits.
