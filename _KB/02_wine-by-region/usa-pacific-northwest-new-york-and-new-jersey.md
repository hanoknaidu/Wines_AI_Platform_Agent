# USA, Oregon, Washington, New York and New Jersey

These regions matter for two reasons: they are top sources of cool-climate and value-premium wine, and **New York and New Jersey are local**, which customers increasingly ask for ("local wine", "made in NY", "Jersey wine").

## 1. Oregon (`USA>Oregon`)

- **Willamette Valley:** Oregon's heart; **Pinot Noir** (elegant, red cherry, earthy, between Burgundy and California in style), **Pinot Gris** (richer than Italian Grigio), **Chardonnay** (rising star). Sub-AVAs include Dundee Hills, Eola-Amity Hills, Ribbon Ridge, Yamhill-Carlton, Chehalem Mountains, McMinnville, Van Duzer Corridor.
- **Southern Oregon (Rogue, Umpqua, Applegate Valleys):** warmer; Tempranillo, Syrah, Rhône grapes.
- **Labeling:** Oregon requires a higher varietal minimum than federal law (90% for most varieties named on the label, with a list of exceptions at 75%). "Oregon Pinot Noir" is therefore usually close to 100% Pinot.
- **Recommend for:** Pinot lovers priced out of Burgundy; salmon; Thanksgiving.

## 2. Washington (`USA>Washington`)

- **Columbia Valley** (huge umbrella AVA) with sub-AVAs: **Walla Walla Valley** (shared with Oregon), **Red Mountain** (powerful Cabernet), **Yakima Valley**, **Horse Heaven Hills**, **Wahluke Slope**.
- **Grapes:** **Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Syrah**, Bordeaux blends; **Riesling** (dry and off-dry; one of the largest US Riesling sources), Chardonnay.
- **Style:** Desert-dry, sunny days and cool nights. Ripe fruit with fresher acidity than many Californian reds. Strong **value** in Step-up and Premium.
- **Recommend for:** "Napa-style Cab for less", Merlot lovers, Riesling.

## 3. New York State (`USA>New York`), local for NY customers

| Region | Highlights |
|---|---|
| **Finger Lakes AVA** (incl. Seneca Lake and Cayuga Lake AVAs) | Deep glacial lakes moderate the cold climate. World-class **dry and off-dry Riesling**, Cabernet Franc, Pinot Noir, Gewürztraminer, Grüner Veltliner, traditional-method sparkling. Historic producers pioneered vinifera (European grape) growing in the region. |
| **Long Island** (North Fork of Long Island AVA, The Hamptons Long Island AVA) | Maritime climate. **Merlot**, **Cabernet Franc**, Bordeaux blends, Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay, and very popular **dry rosé**. |
| **Hudson Valley** | One of the oldest wine regions in the US; home to one of the country's oldest continuously operating wineries. Seyval Blanc, Cabernet Franc, hybrids, cider. |
| **Niagara Escarpment, Lake Erie, Champlain Valley** | Riesling, ice wine, hybrids; Lake Erie is a major Concord grape area (juice, sweet wines). |

**Hybrids and native grapes:** Many NY wines use cold-hardy hybrids (Seyval Blanc, Vidal Blanc, Baco Noir, Marquette) or native labrusca grapes (Concord, Niagara, Catawba), which often make sweeter, grapey wines. Describe these honestly: "a local style that's sweeter and grapey".

## 4. New Jersey (`USA>New Jersey`), local for NJ customers

| AVA | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Outer Coastal Plain** | Largest NJ AVA, in the south; sandy soils, maritime influence. Chambourcin, Cabernet Franc, Merlot, Chardonnay, Albariño and others. |
| **Cape May Peninsula** | Southern tip, surrounded by water; warmest growing season in NJ. |
| **Warren Hills** | Northwest NJ; cooler, hillier. |
| **Central Delaware Valley** | Shared with Pennsylvania along the Delaware River. |

- **Grapes:** Vinifera (Cabernet Franc, Chardonnay, Merlot, Riesling) plus hybrids (**Chambourcin**, Vidal Blanc, Traminette) and fruit wines.
- NJ has a long winemaking history (one of the oldest US wineries still operating is in southern NJ) and a growing quality reputation; several NJ wines have won attention in blind tastings against European wines.
- **Recommend for:** "local wine" requests, gifts for NJ pride, Cabernet Franc and Chambourcin as easy reds.

**Local-wine availability rules (A-01):** a NY-made wine sold to a NJ customer must be in the **NJ Store's** inventory, and vice versa. Always check `check_inventory` with the customer's fulfilling location. Some small local wineries sell only at their tasting rooms or through their own direct-shipping licenses. If we don't carry one, offer a product request ([special-orders-and-product-requests.md](../09_stock-and-availability/special-orders-and-product-requests.md)).

## 5. Other US regions (brief)

- **Virginia:** Viognier, Cabernet Franc, Petit Manseng; Bordeaux blends.
- **Texas (Texas Hill Country, High Plains):** Tempranillo, Rhône and Mediterranean grapes.
- **Michigan, Pennsylvania, Idaho, Arizona, Colorado:** emerging; assortment varies.

## 6. Indicative price bands

- **Everyday:** Washington Riesling and Merlot (large producers), NY/NJ hybrids and fruit wines.
- **Step-up:** Willamette Pinot Gris and entry Pinot Noir, Columbia Valley Cabernet, Finger Lakes Riesling, Long Island rosé and Merlot, NJ Cabernet Franc.
- **Premium:** Willamette sub-AVA Pinot, Walla Walla/Red Mountain Cabernet and Syrah, top Finger Lakes and Long Island reds, traditional-method sparkling.
- **Luxury/Collectible:** Washington cult Cabernet producers, top Oregon single-vineyard Pinot.
