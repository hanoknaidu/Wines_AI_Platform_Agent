# Champagne Houses and Sparkling Wine Brands

> Background knowledge for recognizing brands and inferring style. **Availability, price and exact cuvées come from tools.** House styles describe the flagship non-vintage Brut unless stated. Ownership is omitted on purpose because it changes; see BRD-R5.

## 1. Major Champagne houses (Grandes Marques)

| House | Based in | Flagship NV | House style (plain words) | Prestige / notable cuvées | Typical band |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Moët & Chandon** | Épernay | Impérial Brut | Bright, fruity, approachable; the world's best-known Champagne | Dom Pérignon (separate prestige brand, vintage only); Rosé Impérial; Nectar Impérial (sweeter) | Premium |
| **Veuve Clicquot** | Reims | Yellow Label Brut | Pinot Noir-led, fuller, toasty, rich | La Grande Dame; Vintage; Rosé | Premium |
| **Dom Pérignon** | Épernay | (vintage only) | Complex, creamy, toasty | P2 (later-release) | Collectible |
| **Taittinger** | Reims | Brut La Française | Chardonnay-rich, elegant, light, floral | Comtes de Champagne Blanc de Blancs | Premium |
| **Laurent-Perrier** | Tours-sur-Marne | La Cuvée | Fresh, delicate, Chardonnay-driven | Cuvée Rosé (famous rosé made by maceration); Grand Siècle | Premium |
| **Bollinger** | Aÿ | Special Cuvée | Pinot Noir-dominant, rich, nutty; barrel fermentation | La Grande Année; R.D. | Premium–Luxury |
| **Louis Roederer** | Reims | Collection (numbered multi-vintage blend) | Rich, precise, fine | Cristal; Vintage | Premium; Cristal Collectible |
| **Krug** | Reims | Grande Cuvée (multi-vintage, edition-numbered) | Very rich, complex, long-aged, oak-fermented | Vintage; Clos du Mesnil | Luxury–Collectible |
| **Pol Roger** | Épernay | Brut Réserve ("White Foil") | Balanced, elegant | Cuvée Sir Winston Churchill | Premium |
| **Ruinart** | Reims | Blanc de Blancs (also "R" de Ruinart) | Chardonnay-focused, fresh, citrus | Dom Ruinart | Premium–Luxury |
| **Perrier-Jouët** | Épernay | Grand Brut | Floral, delicate | Belle Époque (Art Nouveau anemone bottle) | Premium; Belle Époque Luxury |
| **G.H. Mumm** | Reims | Cordon Rouge | Fresh, fruity, Pinot-led | RSRV range | Premium |
| **Piper-Heidsieck** | Reims | Cuvée Brut | Fruity, crisp | Rare | Premium |
| **Charles Heidsieck** | Reims | Brut Réserve | Rich, toasty, high reserve-wine share | Blanc des Millénaires | Premium |
| **Billecart-Salmon** | Mareuil-sur-Aÿ | Brut Réserve | Elegant, fresh | Brut Rosé (benchmark rosé) | Premium |
| **Nicolas Feuillatte** | Chouilly | Réserve Exclusive Brut | Fresh, fruity; one of the most popular in France | Palmes d'Or | Step-up–Premium |
| **Lanson, Pommery, Henriot, Deutz, Gosset, Philipponnat, Bruno Paillard, Drappier, Jacquart** | Various | — | Range from fresh/crisp (Lanson) to rich (Gosset) | — | Premium |

**Grower Champagne (RM):** small producers who grow their own grapes, e.g., Pierre Péters, Egly-Ouriet, Larmandier-Bernier, Vilmart, Pierre Gimonnet, Chartogne-Taillet, Jacques Selosse (collectible). Popular with enthusiasts for terroir expression. Check the RM/NM code (see [champagne.md](../04_sparkling-champagne-prosecco/champagne.md)).

## 3. Cava brands

| Brand | Style | Band |
|---|---|---|
| **Freixenet** (Cordon Negro, black frosted bottle) | Crisp, citrus, value | Everyday |
| **Segura Viudas** (Brut Reserva) | Crisp, toasty hint | Everyday |
| **Codorníu** | Historic Cava pioneer; fruity | Everyday–Step-up |
| **Juvé & Camps, Gramona, Recaredo** | Long-aged, premium | Step-up–Premium |

Note: some top Penedès producers left the Cava DO for their own designations (e.g., Corpinnat, Clàssic Penedès). The label may not say "Cava" even though the style is similar.

## 5. "Something like…" quick map

| Customer likes | Suggest (style match) |
|---|---|
| Veuve Clicquot (rich, Pinot-led) | Bollinger, Charles Heidsieck, Mumm, Roederer Estate (US value) |
| Moët Impérial (fruity, approachable) | Nicolas Feuillatte, Piper-Heidsieck, Domaine Chandon (value) |
| Taittinger / Laurent-Perrier (light, Chardonnay-driven) | Ruinart Blanc de Blancs, Blanc de Blancs Crémant, Pierre Gimonnet (grower) |
| La Marca (fruity Prosecco) | Mionetto, Ruffino, Zonin; DOCG step-up: Nino Franco, Adami |
| Champagne taste, lower budget | Crémant (de Bourgogne, d'Alsace, de Loire, de Limoux), Cava Reserva, Gruet, Roederer Estate |

## 6. Notes for the agent

- Vintage Champagne and prestige cuvées are **vintage-specific** (A-23); never substitute.
- Gift boxes, magnums and limited editions are separate SKUs. Search them specifically.
- "Nectar", "Demi-Sec", "Rich" cuvées are **sweeter**. Check `sweetness`.
