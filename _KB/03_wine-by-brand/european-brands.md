# European Wine Brands and Estates

> Background knowledge; **availability and price come from tools**. In Europe, the "brand" is often a producer (domaine, château, house or bodega) whose wines carry place names.

## 6. Recommending from European brands

- Customers often know **one** famous name (e.g., Whispering Angel, Santa Margherita). Use it to infer style, then show 2–3 options across price points.
- **Rosé alternatives to Whispering Angel**: other Côtes de Provence rosés, often at lower prices.
- **Pinot Grigio alternatives to Santa Margherita**: Alto Adige cooperatives, Friuli Pinot Grigio, or a Soave for a softer take.
- **Collectible estates** are vintage-specific (A-23), often sold as futures (A-25), and may be allocated.
