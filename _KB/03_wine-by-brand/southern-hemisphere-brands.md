# Southern Hemisphere Wine Brands

> Background knowledge; **availability and price come from tools.**

## 1. New Zealand

| Brand | Known for | Style | Band |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kim Crawford** | Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc; also "Illuminate" lower-alcohol/lower-calorie range | Zesty grapefruit, passion fruit, very popular | Everyday–Step-up |
| **Oyster Bay** | Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Noir | Crisp, bright | Everyday |
| **Cloudy Bay** | Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc (a benchmark that helped make the style famous), Te Koko (oaked), Pinot Noir | Refined, intense | Step-up–Premium |
| **Brancott Estate** | Marlborough pioneer (planted the region's first commercial Sauvignon Blanc vines in the 1970s) | Classic Marlborough | Everyday |
| **Villa Maria, Whitehaven, Matua, Nobilo, Giesen** | Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Noir; Giesen also makes 0% (non-alcoholic) wines | Bright | Everyday |
| **Felton Road, Rippon, Mt. Difficulty** | Central Otago Pinot Noir | Elegant to intense | Premium |
| **Craggy Range** | Hawke's Bay, Martinborough | Pinot, Bordeaux blends, Syrah | Step-up–Premium |

## 2. Australia

| Brand | Known for | Style | Band |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[yellow tail]** | Shiraz, Chardonnay, Moscato, many varietals | Simple, fruity, very approachable; one of the best-selling imported brands in the US | Everyday |
| **Penfolds** | Grange (iconic Shiraz), Bin 389, Bin 28, Bin 407, Koonunga Hill | Powerful, polished; numbered "Bin" wines | Everyday (Koonunga Hill) – Collectible (Grange) |
| **Jacob's Creek, Lindeman's, Wolf Blass, Rosemount** | Value varietals | Fruity | Everyday |
| **19 Crimes** | Red blends (Australian and Californian versions) | Bold, smooth | Everyday |
| **Two Hands, Torbreck, Mollydooker, Henschke, Yalumba** | Barossa/Eden/McLaren Vale Shiraz, Grenache | Rich to elegant; Henschke's Hill of Grace is iconic | Step-up–Collectible |
| **d'Arenberg** | McLaren Vale (The Footbolt, The Dead Arm) | Rich, quirky names | Step-up–Premium |
| **Leeuwin Estate, Vasse Felix, Cullen** | Margaret River Chardonnay and Cabernet | Elegant | Premium–Luxury |
| **Grosset, Pewsey Vale** | Clare/Eden Valley dry Riesling | Lime, steely | Step-up |
