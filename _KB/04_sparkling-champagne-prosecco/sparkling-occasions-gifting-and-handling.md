# Sparkling Wine for Occasions, Quantities, Gifting and Handling

## 1. Party quantity guidance

**Responsible framing (G8):** quantities are planning estimates for a host, never encouragement to drink more. A standard 750 ml bottle pours about **five 5-oz glasses** or **six to eight 4-oz flutes**.

| Occasion | Planning estimate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Toast only** (one glass each) | 1 bottle per 6–8 guests | Use flutes; 3–4 oz pours |
| **Cocktail party, 2–3 hours, sparkling only** | ~1 bottle per 2 guests | Assume ~2 drinks per guest in the first hour, ~1 per hour after |
| **Sparkling as one of several drinks** | ~1 bottle per 4 guests | Mix with still wine, beer, non-alcoholic |
| **Wedding: toast + reception** | Toast: 1 bottle per 8 guests; reception wine overall ~1 bottle per 2 guests over the evening | Split among sparkling, white, red based on menu |
| **Brunch mimosas** | ~1 bottle per 3 guests | ~8 mimosas per bottle at 3 oz sparkling each |
| **New Year's Eve midnight toast** | 1 bottle per 6 guests for the toast | |

Always include **non-alcoholic options** for guests who don't drink, are driving, or are under 21 (see folder 07; NJ Store only, A-06).

**Mixed-case discount:** 6+ bottles = 10%, 12+ = 15% (A-50). Mention once, at the moment it's useful.

**Large orders:** above $2,500 or 60 bottles, route to the events team (A-26) via `create_support_ticket(category="bulk_order", priority="P2")`. See [purchase-limits-and-bulk-orders.md](../09_stock-and-availability/purchase-limits-and-bulk-orders.md) and [events-tastings-and-corporate-orders.md](../14_general-faqs/events-tastings-and-corporate-orders.md).

## 3. Gifting

- **Gift-ready options:** gift boxes from the producer (separate SKUs), magnums, gift notes (see [gift-orders.md](../11_orders-delivery-pickup/gift-orders.md)).
- **Recipient must be 21+ and present with ID** at a NJ/NY address (G4, G5).
- **Safe choices when taste is unknown:** recognizable NV Brut Champagne (Premium), a DOCG Prosecco (Step-up), a Champagne rosé for romantic occasions.
- **Corporate gifts:** route bulk (10+ recipients) to the corporate team.

## 4. Chilling and serving

| Topic | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Serving temperature | 43–48°F (6–9°C). Vintage/prestige Champagne slightly warmer, 48–50°F |
| Fast chilling | Ice bucket with ice **and water** (plus a handful of salt): ~15–20 minutes. Fridge: ~3 hours. Freezer: max 15–20 minutes, then take it out. Bottles left in the freezer can burst |
| Glassware | Tulip or white-wine glasses show aroma better than narrow flutes; coupes look festive but lose bubbles faster |
| Leftover bubbly | A sparkling stopper in the fridge keeps it lively for about 1–3 days |

## 5. Opening safely (share proactively at parties)

Sparkling bottles are under high pressure (about 5–6 atmospheres in Champagne, roughly 3× a car tire). Corks can cause eye injuries.

1. **Chill well**: warm bottles are more likely to gush and pop.
2. Remove the foil. **Keep a thumb on the cork** from the moment you loosen the cage (usually 6 half-turns).
3. **Point the bottle away** from people, faces and light fixtures, at about 45°.
4. Hold the cork and **turn the bottle**, not the cork. Ease it out with a gentle sigh, not a pop.
5. Never use a corkscrew on a sparkling wine cork.

If someone is injured by a cork or broken glass, the agent should express care, recommend seeking medical attention if needed, and escalate P1 (`safety_crisis`) if the injury relates to a product defect.

## 6. Storage

- Store bottles **cool, dark, steady temperature (~55°F), lying down** or upright for short periods; avoid kitchen heat and sunlight.
- NV sparkling is ready to drink on release; Prosecco is best within 1–2 years of vintage.
- **Shipping heat:** carbonated wines are especially sensitive to heat in transit. Weather holds (A-19) apply.

## 7. Sample dialogues

> **Customer:** How much Champagne do I need for a 50-person wedding toast?
> **Agent:** For a toast with one glass each, plan on about 7 bottles (roughly 7–8 toast pours per bottle). Get 8 to be safe. If you'll also serve sparkling during the reception, you'll want more. Want me to suggest a split between Champagne for the head table and Prosecco or Cava for everyone else? Eight bottles gets you the 10% mixed-case discount.

> **Customer:** I'm having 30 people for New Year's. How much bubbly?
> **Agent:** For a midnight toast, about 5 bottles. If bubbly is the main drink through the evening alongside other options, plan around 8–10 bottles. And please keep some non-alcoholic options and water handy for guests who are driving. Delivery windows for Dec 31 fill quickly, so I'd recommend booking now. Shall I check slots for your ZIP?
