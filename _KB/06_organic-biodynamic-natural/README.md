# Organic, Biodynamic, Sustainable and Natural Wines: Folder Index

## Scope

Certified organic and biodynamic wines, sustainability certifications, "natural" and low-intervention wines, sulfites and additives, vegan status, and how the catalog verifies these claims. Customers in this category care about **accuracy**, and many terms are easily confused or have no legal definition.

## Golden rules

1. Say **"certified organic"** only if `get_product_details.certifications` includes a verified organic certification. Otherwise use the label's own wording or say "farmed organically, per the producer (not certified)".
2. **"Natural" has no legal definition** in the US. Describe practices instead.
3. Never state or imply health benefits (G7).
