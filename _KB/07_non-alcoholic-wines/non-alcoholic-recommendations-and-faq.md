# Non-Alcoholic Wine Recommendations and FAQ

> All recommendations assume the NJ Store and a 21+ customer (NAW-R1, NAW-R2).

## 1. By occasion

| Occasion | Recommend | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Toasts (weddings, showers, New Year's) | NA sparkling Brut or rosé | Bubbles deliver the celebratory feel and the best texture |
| Dinner party alongside wine | NA aromatic white (Riesling, Sauvignon Blanc) or rosé | Pairs well; feels like wine at the table |
| Steak / hearty dinner | NA red, served slightly chilled; set expectations | Reds are lighter than alcoholic versions |
| "Dry January", "Sober October" | Mixed pack: sparkling, white, rosé | Variety keeps it interesting; mention the mixed-case discount for 6+ (A-50) |
| Designated driver gift / host gift | Premium NA sparkling (gift-worthy bottle) | Thoughtful inclusion |
| Brunch | NA sparkling for NA mimosas | Juice masks lighter body |
| Corporate events | NA sparkling + NA white | Inclusive options; see corporate orders |
| Spicy food | NA Riesling / Muscat (often slightly sweet) | Sweetness tames heat |

## 2. Pairing tips

- NA wines are lighter, so pair with **lighter dishes**: salads, seafood, chicken, cheese plates, sushi, spicy Asian dishes.
- Serve **colder** than you would the alcoholic version; it sharpens flavors.
- Sparkling NA works with almost anything salty or fried.

## 3. FAQ

**Q: Is non-alcoholic wine completely alcohol-free?**
"Non-alcoholic" or "dealcoholized" means under 0.5% alcohol, about the level in some fruit juices or kombucha. "Alcohol-free" or "0.0%" means no detectable alcohol. We label each product clearly.

**Q: Can I get drunk from it?**
At under 0.5% ABV it's considered non-alcoholic, but we can't advise on effects. Some people, including those who are pregnant or in recovery, avoid all trace alcohol; 0.0% options are available.

**Q: Why does it taste sweeter?**
Removing alcohol removes body and warmth, so some producers add a little grape juice or sugar for balance. Check `sugar_g` on the label for each wine.

**Q: Does NA wine have fewer calories?**
Usually, yes, often far fewer, because alcohol is the main source of calories in wine. Sugar content varies. Check Nutrition Facts.

**Q: How long does it last after opening?**
About 2–5 days in the fridge with a stopper (1–3 days for sparkling). It spoils faster than regular wine.

**Q: Why can't I buy it under 21?**
It's our store policy: we're a licensed wine shop, and we apply the same 21+ rule to every product.

**Q: Why can't I get it delivered in New York?**
New York rules currently restrict what wine and liquor stores can sell, including non-alcoholic versions of wine. Our NJ store can sell them for NJ delivery or pickup.

**Q: Is NA wine "healthy"?**
We can't make health claims. It has no (or trace) alcohol and usually fewer calories; nutrition details are on each label.

**Q: Can I return it if I don't like the taste?**
NJ Store returns rules apply: unopened, within 14 days, with a 15% restocking fee or full store credit (A-34). Defective or past-best-by products are always covered.
