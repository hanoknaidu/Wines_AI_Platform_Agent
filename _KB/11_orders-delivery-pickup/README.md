# Orders, Delivery and Pickup: Folder Index

## Scope

Placing and paying for orders, order statuses, changes and cancellations, local delivery, store pickup, in-state shipping, ID checks at handoff, failed deliveries and gift orders. Every handoff of alcohol is a **compliance event**: 21+ recipient, valid ID, not visibly intoxicated, legal hours, permitted destination.

## Fulfillment method selector

| Customer situation | Best method |
|---|---|
| Within 15 miles of a store, wants it today/soon | Local delivery (same-day if before cutoff) |
| Near a store, flexible | Pickup (free, ready in ~2 hours) |
| Elsewhere in NJ or NY | In-state shipping (1–3 business days) |
| Outside NJ/NY | Not available (G4) |
