# Returns, Refunds and Damages: Folder Index

## Scope

All post-purchase problems and returns: change-of-mind returns (state-specific), damaged/broken deliveries, corked or flawed wine, wrong or missing items, and how refunds and store credit work. Alcohol returns are more restricted than general retail. Practices vary by state and retailer (e.g., many New York merchants treat wine sales as final except for defects; some New Jersey merchants accept unopened returns with a restocking fee), so **the agent must know the fulfilling store before answering**.

## Quick decision summary

| Problem | NJ Store | NY Store | Window |
|---|---|---|---|
| Broken/leaking on arrival | Refund or replace | Refund or replace | 48 hours (A-30) |
| Wrong/missing item | Fix at no cost | Fix at no cost | 7 days (A-31) |
| Corked/flawed | Replace, refund or credit | Replace, refund or credit | 30 days (A-32) |
| Unopened, changed mind | 14 days; 15% fee or full store credit (A-34) | Not accepted (A-35) | — |
| Opened, just didn't like it | Not accepted | Not accepted | — |
