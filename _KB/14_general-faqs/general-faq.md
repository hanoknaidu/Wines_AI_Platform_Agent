# General FAQ

Short answers to the most common questions. Each links to the full policy. Live facts (prices, stock, hours, fees for an address) always come from tools.

## Ordering and delivery

1. **Where do you deliver?** Addresses in New Jersey and New York only: local delivery within ~15 miles of our stores, and shipping elsewhere in those states (A-02). → [local-delivery-policy.md](../11_orders-delivery-pickup/local-delivery-policy.md), [in-state-shipping-policy.md](../11_orders-delivery-pickup/in-state-shipping-policy.md)
2. **Can you ship to another state?** No. State alcohol laws limit us to NJ and NY. → [compliance-guardrails.md](../00_system/compliance-guardrails.md) G4
3. **How much is delivery?** Local: $5.99 or $9.99 by zone, free over $99; shipping from $14.99, free over $199 (A-10, A-12, A-15). Exact fees for your address at checkout.
4. **Can I get it today?** Usually, if you order by 6 pm (3 pm Sunday) and a window is open (A-13).
5. **Do I have to be home?** Yes, someone 21+ with valid ID must receive it. We can't leave alcohol at the door. → [age-verification-at-handoff.md](../11_orders-delivery-pickup/age-verification-at-handoff.md)
6. **Can I pick up instead?** Yes, free, usually ready in about 2 hours and held 7 days (A-16). → [pickup-policy.md](../11_orders-delivery-pickup/pickup-policy.md)
7. **Can someone else pick up my order?** Yes, name an alternate pickup person; they need a 21+ ID matching that name.
8. **Can I cancel?** Free before we start packing (A-21). → [order-changes-and-cancellations.md](../11_orders-delivery-pickup/order-changes-and-cancellations.md)
9. **Where's my order?** I can check. What's your order number? → [order-status-lifecycle.md](../11_orders-delivery-pickup/order-status-lifecycle.md)
10. **Can I send wine as a gift?** Yes, to NJ/NY addresses with a 21+ recipient present, with a gift note and no prices on the slip. → [gift-orders.md](../11_orders-delivery-pickup/gift-orders.md)

## Age and ID

11. **Why do you need my ID?** We're required to sell only to adults 21+. We verify once online and again at handoff. → [account-creation-and-age-verification.md](../13_account-management/account-creation-and-age-verification.md)
12. **What IDs do you accept?** Valid, unexpired government photo ID: driver's license, state ID, passport/passport card, military ID. → handoff doc
13. **Do I need to be 21 for non-alcoholic wine?** Yes, our policy is 21+ for every product (A-04).

14. **Do you have [wine]?** Let me check stock at your nearest store. → [inventory-status-definitions.md](../09_stock-and-availability/inventory-status-definitions.md)

16. **Which vintage will I get?** The listed one for vintage-specific wines; adjacent vintages may be supplied for everyday wines under $25. → [vintage-policy.md](../09_stock-and-availability/vintage-policy.md)
17. **Can you get a wine you don't carry?** Often. I can submit a request or special order. → [special-orders-and-product-requests.md](../09_stock-and-availability/special-orders-and-product-requests.md)
18. **Do you have organic / vegan / low-sulfite wines?** Yes. → folder [06](../06_organic-biodynamic-natural/README.md)
19. **Do you have low-calorie wines?** Yes, with label calorie info where available. → folder [05](../05_low-calorie-and-better-for-you/README.md)
20. **Do you have non-alcoholic wine?** Yes, at our NJ store (not available for NY delivery right now) (A-06). → folder [07](../07_non-alcoholic-wines/README.md)
21. **Champagne vs Prosecco?** → [sparkling-styles-and-production-methods.md](../04_sparkling-champagne-prosecco/sparkling-styles-and-production-methods.md)
22. **What's a good wine for…?** Tell me the occasion, food or what you usually like. → [taste-discovery-conversation-flow.md](../01_wine-by-taste/taste-discovery-conversation-flow.md)
23. **Do you sell kosher wine?** Yes. Kosher, Kosher for Passover and Mevushal are listed separately.

## Prices and discounts

24. **Are online prices the same as in store?** Yes, for the same store, except channel-specific promotions. → [pricing-policy.md](../08_pricing/pricing-policy.md)
25. **Do you have case discounts?** 10% off 6+ eligible wines, 15% off 12+, mix and match (A-50). → [mixed-case-and-volume-discounts.md](../10_discounts-and-promotions/mixed-case-and-volume-discounts.md)
26. **Do you price match?** Sometimes: identical item, licensed retailer in the same state, human-approved. → [price-matching-policy.md](../08_pricing/price-matching-policy.md)
27. **My code doesn't work.** → [discount-troubleshooting.md](../10_discounts-and-promotions/discount-troubleshooting.md)
28. **How does Rewards work?** Free; 1 point per $1; 250 points = $10 (A-53). → [rewards-loyalty-program.md](../10_discounts-and-promotions/rewards-loyalty-program.md)
29. **Do gift cards expire?** Never; no fees (A-56). → [gift-cards.md](gift-cards.md)

## Returns and problems

30. **A bottle arrived broken.** Report within 48 hours with a photo, and we'll refund or replace (A-30). → [damaged-or-broken-items.md](../12_returns-refunds-damages/damaged-or-broken-items.md)
31. **My wine tastes off / corked.** Keep the bottle with the wine in it and report within 30 days (A-32). → [corked-or-flawed-wine.md](../12_returns-refunds-damages/corked-or-flawed-wine.md)
32. **I got the wrong wine.** Report within 7 days; we'll fix it at no cost (A-31). → [wrong-or-missing-items.md](../12_returns-refunds-damages/wrong-or-missing-items.md)
33. **Can I return unopened wine?** NJ store: within 14 days, 15% fee or full store credit (A-34). NY store: no, unless faulty or our error (A-35). → [returns-policy.md](../12_returns-refunds-damages/returns-policy.md)
34. **When will I get my refund?** Cards: 5–10 business days after it's issued; store credit: instant. → [refunds-and-store-credit.md](../12_returns-refunds-damages/refunds-and-store-credit.md)
